# Photo references for sample products

Metadata and original image URLs are recorded per product in `products/demo_catalog_photos.json`.

Source: Fashion Product Images dataset, originally collected from Myntra by Param Aggarwal. CSV metadata mirror: https://github.com/dcupl-demos/fashion-products/tree/main/dcupl/data . Original dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/paramaggarwal/fashion-product-images-dataset .

The photographs are reference images for synthetic demonstration listings. They do not depict items owned or offered for sale by Gaia users. Image rights remain with their respective owners. No open license or transfer of image rights is claimed by this repository.
